## Pathology check (spec §32) — Finding PERF‑1 (LOW/INFO · non‑blocking)

¹ The two mutating ops show `rerunMeanDrift > 1.5`: batch 2 is ~3× slower than batch 1. A
targeted probe (16 000 consecutive `inc` invokes against one `createMemoryPersistence`
authority) shows per‑invoke latency rising **~linearly** — 0.009 → 0.044 ms over 16 000
commits (≈ 4.7×), heap flat at ~33 MB. This is the **in‑memory persistence adapter's
un‑compacted revision log** growing within a single long‑lived process: **linear, not
O(n²)**; heap bounded; `snapshot` (which also accumulates) shows drift 1.08 — flat.
Durable adapters (`createSqlitePersistence`) are unaffected, and the in‑memory adapter is a
dev/test facility (`ProviderCapabilities.mutationObservation` doc: "the memory reference …
single‑process anyway"). No unbounded growth, no authorization‑cost explosion, no
workflow‑history or live‑query‑subscriber scaling surprise was found. Recommend a one‑line
note in `docs/STORAGE.md`.
